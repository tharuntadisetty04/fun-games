The Paradox: Your intuition says switching vs. staying is 50/50 — but it's not. When you first pick, you have a 1/3 chance of being right. That means there's a 2/3 chance the car is behind one of the other doors. When Monty opens a goat door, that 2/3 probability collapses entirely onto the remaining unopened door.
